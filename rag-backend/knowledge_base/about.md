# About Agni College of Technology (ACT)

Agni College of Technology (ACT) is a NAAC A+ accredited, autonomous engineering
institution located on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), Thalambur, Chennai 600130,
Tamil Nadu. It is affiliated with Anna University and approved by AICTE.

- **Founded:** 2001
- **Run by:** Agni Group, a first-generation entrepreneurial venture with
  diversified business interests spanning over 25 years.
- **Accreditation:** NAAC A+ (institution-level). NBA accreditation for select
  UG departments (ECE, EEE, CSE).
- **Autonomous status:** ACT is an autonomous institution, meaning it designs
  and updates its own curriculum under Anna University's academic framework.

## Vision
To be a premier institution producing technically competent, ethical, and
industry-ready engineers who contribute meaningfully to society.

## Campus Facilities
- Library with 71,670+ volumes plus digital journal access
- Well-equipped labs: computer, physics, chemistry, life sciences, and
  department-specific labs
- Sports complex: cricket, football, volleyball, athletics track
- Gym and yoga facilities
- Hostel accommodation (separate for boys and girls)
- Campus transport network connecting Chennai/OMR and nearby areas
- Health center and cafeteria
- 18 Centres of Excellence supporting industry-aligned learning
