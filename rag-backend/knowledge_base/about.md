# About Agni College of Technology

Agni College of Technology is a NAAC A+ accredited, autonomous engineering
institution on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR), Thalambur, Chennai 600130,
Tamil Nadu (about 20 km from Adyar, on the IT Highway). Affiliated to Anna
University, approved by AICTE, ISO 9001:2015 certified. It holds 2(f), 12(B)
and autonomous status.

- **Founded:** 2001, by Sri Balaji Charitable and Educational Trust
- **Managed by:** the Agni family / Agni Group, a first-generation
  entrepreneurial venture
- **Accreditation:** NAAC A+; NBA accredited (the website says 100% of eligible
  programmes are NBA accredited; ECE, EEE and CSE are confirmed NBA-accredited)
- **Counselling Code (TNEA):** 1316

## Vision
To be one among the top institutions offering engineering and technology
education by training, creating and nurturing an enterprising, innovative and
environmental spirit among students, for continual growth as professionals
with values.
