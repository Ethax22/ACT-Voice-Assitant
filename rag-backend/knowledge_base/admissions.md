# Admissions at Agni

Source: official admissions page (act.edu.in/admissions-agni), 2026-27.

## Undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech)
- Primarily through **TNEA** counselling on Class 12 marks per Anna University
  norms. **TNEA Counselling Code: 1316**
- Eligibility: as prescribed by Anna University. No age limit.
- Lateral entry (diploma / HSC vocational): average minimum 50% (OC), 45% (BC),
  40% (MBC) in Maths, Physics, Chemistry. SC/ST: a pass in the qualifying exam
  is sufficient.
- Application categories: B.E/B.Tech, B.E/B.Tech lateral entry, B.E lateral
  entry for working professionals, and M.E (PG).
- Management quota seats may be available — contact admissions for current
  availability and fees.

## Postgraduate (M.E.)
- Based on **GATE** or **TANCET/CEETA** counselling per Anna University.
- Only **M.E. Structural Engineering** is currently listed.

## How to Apply
- Online via the Agni website (act.edu.in online application) or the TNEA
  single-window portal for UG.
- Application form: Rs 300 (cash/DD), Rs 350 if requested by post. DD payable to
  "Agni College of Technology", Chennai.

## Documents needed
Original mark sheets, Transfer and Conduct Certificates, community certificate
(BC/MBC/SC/ST), Anna University eligibility certificate (non-Tamil Nadu
students), 5 passport photos, SSLC mark sheet (date-of-birth proof), and a
student/guardian conduct declaration.

## Fees policy
- Fees are paid before the start of each semester, with a 15-day grace period;
  late fines apply. Fees paid are not refundable. Non-payment after the grace
  period can bar a student from class, hostel and exams.
- Exact fee amounts are not published on the site — direct to admissions.

## Scholarships
- A Merit Scholarship exists (application form on the site). Agni reports 4,000+
  scholarships awarded over five years; confirm the current scheme with
  admissions.

## Attendance rule
Minimum 75% attendance (excluding OD) to sit university exams; no condonation
for medical leave.
