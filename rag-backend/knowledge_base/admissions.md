# Admissions at ACT

## Undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech) Admission
- Admission to UG programs is primarily through **TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering
  Admissions)** counselling, based on Class 12 marks (cutoff) as per Anna
  University norms.
- **TNEA Counselling Code for ACT: 1316**
- Management quota seats may also be available — contact admissions directly
  for current availability and fee structure.

## Postgraduate (M.E./M.Tech) Admission
- PG admission is generally based on **GATE** score or **TANCET/CEETA**
  counselling, per Anna University's PG admission process.

## How to Apply
- Apply online via the official ACT admissions portal (act.edu.in) or through
  the TNEA single-window counselling portal for UG.
- For direct/management quota queries, contact the admissions hotline.

## Scholarships
- ACT has awarded 4,000+ scholarships over the last 5 years to meritorious
  and needs-based students (exact current-year scheme should be confirmed
  with admissions, as schemes are revised annually).

## Contact for Admissions

- **Email:** mail@act.edu.in
- **TNEA Counselling Code:** 1316

## Important Note for the Assistant
Cutoff marks, exact fee structures, and application deadlines change every
academic year. If a visitor asks for this-year's exact cutoff or fee number
and it is not confirmed in this knowledge base, say so plainly and direct them
to the admissions hotline/email rather than guessing a figure.
