# Placements at ACT

Source: official ACT website placement statistics page (act.edu.in/placement-statistics)
and list-of-recruiters page.

## Year-by-year statistics (official)
| Year | Companies | Eligible students | Registered | Placed | Offers | Placement % | Avg package | Highest package |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021-22 | 145 | 453 | 424 | 403 | 587 | 95% | 3 LPA | 6 LPA |
| 2022-23 | 112 | 338 | 327 | 297 | 339 | 91% | 3.25 LPA | 8 LPA |
| 2023-24 | 112 | 358 | 348 | 292 | 312 | 84% | 4.2 LPA | 6 LPA |
| 2024-25 | 174 | 389 | 361 | 319 | 424 | 88% | 4.25 LPA | 9 LPA |

- Placement % is students placed out of students registered for placement
  (for 2024-25: 319 of 361 registered).
- The website's overall highlights also cite an all-time highest package of
  **49 LPA**, an all-time average of about 4.6 LPA, and 416+ multiple offers.
  The 49 LPA figure is an all-time best, NOT the 2024-25 highest (9 LPA) —
  never present it as this year's number.
- The recruiters page counts 185 organisations for 2024-25 while the statistics
  page says 174 — say "around 175-185 companies" rather than one exact number.
- Department-wise placement numbers are not published; don't invent them.
