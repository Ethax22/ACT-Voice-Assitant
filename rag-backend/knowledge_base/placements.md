# Placements at ACT

## 2025 Placement Season (as reported publicly)
- **148 recruiters** participated in the placement process
- **523 job offers** were extended to students
- **319 students** were placed in 2025 (up from 292 in 2024)
- Reported highest package: figures vary by source, ranging from roughly
  **INR 9 LPA to INR 49 LPA** depending on the reporting platform — treat the
  exact highest-package figure as approximate and defer to the placement cell
  for the verified number.
- Reported average package: approximately **INR 4.25–4.6 LPA**

## Top Recruiters (indicative list)
TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Capgemini, Zoho, Hitachi, Royal Enfield, Cisco, and
other companies across IT, BFSI, FMCG, consulting, and e-commerce sectors.

## Placement Support
- 18 Centres of Excellence help align student skills with industry needs
- Dedicated placement/training cell coordinates recruiter drives, mock
  interviews, and aptitude/soft-skills training

## Important Note for the Assistant
Placement statistics change every year and third-party sources sometimes
disagree on exact figures (e.g., highest package). When answering, give the
general trend/range from this document, note that figures are approximate,
and suggest checking with the placement cell or official site for the
verified current-year numbers rather than stating a single figure as
definitive fact.
