## Agni Ventura 2026 - Pitch your ideas contest
Posted on the website: 10 January 2026

The Innovation and Product Development (IPD) Cell of Agni College of Technology successfully organized Agni Ventura 2026 on 10 January 2026, a dynamic platform designed to foster innovation, entrepreneurial thinking, and product-oriented problem solving among students.
This flagship event brought together creative minds from diverse engineering domains to present impactful ideas addressing real-world challenges.
A total of 138 teams registered for Agni Ventura 2026, out of which 50 teams were shortlisted, and the top 15 teams advanced to the final round. The selection process followed a rigorous multi-stage evaluation, including idea screening, pitch deck evaluation, and final presentation before an expert jury. The top 3 teams were awarded prizes for their outstanding innovations.
