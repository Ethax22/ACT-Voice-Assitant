## Centre for Social & Community Services

### Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)

Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) is a flagship initiative of the Ministry of Education, Government of India, designed to harness the knowledge and resources of higher educational institutions for the development of rural India. Agni College of Technology is proud to be a participating institution, committed to bridging the gap between urban and rural development.
Objectives:

Identify and address key developmental challenges in rural communities.
Encourage students and faculty to contribute towards the upliftment of villages through technology and innovation.
Promote sustainable and inclusive growth through community engagement.

Key Activities:

Village adoption programs
Needs assessment and baseline surveys
Clean energy and sanitation projects
Digital literacy and vocational training sessions
Awareness programs on health, nutrition, and environment

Impact:

Through UBA, ACT students learn to empathize with rural issues while applying their academic knowledge to solve real-world problems—empowering both the village and the volunteer.

### National Service Scheme (NSS)

The National Service Scheme (NSS) at Agni College of Technology is a student-led initiative under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports. It encourages students to actively participate in nation-building through community service and outreach programs.

Motto: Not Me, But You

Objectives:

Instill social responsibility and community consciousness in students.
Develop leadership, discipline, and a spirit of volunteerism.
Address local community issues through awareness and action.

Major Activities:

Blood donation and health camps
Tree plantation and environmental drives
Road safety and anti-drug awareness campaigns
Swachh Bharat Abhiyan initiatives
Disaster relief and rehabilitation support

Participation Benefits:

Certificate of participation from the Government of India
Opportunity to work closely with government agencies and NGOs
Platform for personality development and civic engagement

### Youth Red Cross (YRC)

The Youth Red Cross unit at Agni College of Technology functions under the Indian Red Cross Society and Tamil Nadu State Branch. It empowers students to serve humanity and respond to emergencies with compassion and competence.

Vision: To inspire young minds to work for the health and safety of society.

Core Principles:

Protection of life and health
Service to society without discrimination
Promotion of peace and humanitarian values

Key Programs:

First aid training and workshops
Blood donation camps
Health and hygiene awareness campaigns
Community outreach for underprivileged groups
Emergency response and disaster preparedness training

Why Join YRC?

Certificate of participation from the Government of India
Opportunity to work closely with government agencies and NGOs
Platform for personality development and civic engagement

Students gain essential life-saving skills, foster empathy, and learn the value of civic duty, all while building a healthier, more resilient community.

### Rotaract Club

The Rotaract Club of Agni College of Technology is a student chapter of Rotary International’s global movement of young leaders. It provides a platform for students to develop professional skills, engage in community service, and build meaningful connections.

Mission: Fellowship through service.

What We Do:

Organize leadership development programs
Conduct social impact projects and charity drives
Participate in inter-college events and international exchanges
Collaborate with Rotary Clubs on large-scale initiatives
Provide career guidance and soft skills training

Annual Highlights

Literacy drives and student mentoring
Plastic-free campus campaigns
Entrepreneurship workshops
Blood donation and organ donation awareness

Why Rotaract?

Rotaract transforms students into proactive leaders with a heart for service and a vision for impact—locally and globally.

### Personality & Character Development Activity

At Agni College of Technology, Personality & Character Development Activities (PCDA) are a vital part of holistic student development. These programs aim to shape confident, ethical, and socially aware individuals ready to lead in any field.
Objectives:

Enhance communication, leadership, and decision-making skills
Cultivate values such as integrity, discipline, empathy, and resilience
Encourage self-awareness, emotional intelligence, and critical thinking

Core Activities

Public speaking and debate clubs
Leadership bootcamps and motivational talks
Ethics in engineering workshops
Time management and goal-setting sessions
Theatre, team-building games, and reflective journaling

Outcomes

Students emerge with stronger personalities, ready to face real-world challenges with clarity, confidence, and character.
