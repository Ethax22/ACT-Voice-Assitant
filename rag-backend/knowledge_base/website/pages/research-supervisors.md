## Research Supervisors

### Research Supervisors and Recognized Research Centers

In the academic year 2024–25, 50 faculty members at Agni College of Technology hold Ph.D. qualifications. Among them, 10 faculty members are officially recognized as research guides by Anna University, Chennai. Additionally, all departments at Agni College of Technology have Anna University-recognized research centers.

Details of the 50 Ph.D. holders who were actively engaged in research and academic activities during the academic year 2024–25 are documented.

Information on the 10 recognized research guides for the same academic year is also provided. These faculty members are currently mentoring four research scholars pursuing their Ph.D. degrees. Relevant links serving as proof of indexing and official recognition are included for your reference and verification.
