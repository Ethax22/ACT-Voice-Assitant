## IT Support

The world which spins at a rapid pace today needs an organized pattern where time matters a lot! The need for working in an organized way, particularly for storing all relevant data, should be ideal, fast, and easily accessible.

The world of education is rapidly changing, so are the ways of imparting knowledge and the means of storing data in colleges have changed. Using online software is becoming a widely followed trend and has become almost mandatory in college/institutional functioning. Information Technology brings in glad tidings to College Management by introducing what they call CMS.

ITSS has education’s most flexible and interactive scheduling function, thus meeting the communication and information needs of the entire college community in real-time.

Agni College of Technology has now implemented user-friendly Management System software to easily manage and cater to the needs of all departments & functions of the college.

This allows to easily manage the activities of the students, teachers, staff, and events at college. All the functions like students’ enquiries, admissions, registrations, fees collections, timetable, library, attendance, payroll, examination, campus, and canteen can be fed into the system and can be retrieved any time at the click of the button. To maintain a good reputation of the college it is important that information regarding students, their parents, teachers, and staff is quickly available.

The ITSS provides students and parents infallible proof of all their activities, like attendance, performance, participation, and general information that can be fed, maintained and when necessary the same data can be sent through SMS to the parents to apprise them of all relevant information about their wards. This provides both the parents and teachers with the required information.

All the email communication between ITSS and applicants concerning the change of schedules, modules, payments, new events and management information are integrated with the client’s email gateway.

Through the integration of emails and SMS required information can be sent to parents and students. For this purpose Individual login for each student and an Official E-Mail id for each student is created in the ACT.EDU.IN domain.
