## Centre for Training & Skill Development

The Centre for Training and Skill Development is the foundation of our institution’s efforts to prepare students for the competitive and dynamic demands of the modern workforce. Rooted in a commitment to academic and professional excellence, the centre spearheads a wide range of initiatives aimed at fostering technical expertise, analytical acumen, and holistic growth among students.

In addition to technical proficiency, the centre emphasizes preparing students for professional challenges through Mock placement drives, technical interview preparation, and soft skills training. By integrating hands-on experience, real-world applications, and personalized coaching, the centre ensure students develop as competent professionals. Abetted by a collaborative spirit among faculty, industry experts, and students, the centre continues to set new benchmarks in educational and professional development of the student, aligning with evolving industry trends and institutional aspirations.

#### Vision

To emerge as a globally recognized hub for nurturing future-ready engineers by integrating academic excellence, practical exposure, and innovative practices.

#### Mission

Excellence in Training – To set benchmarks in delivering world-class training programs that empower students with cutting-edge competencies.
Global Readiness – To prepare learners to thrive in diverse, multicultural, and technologically evolving work environments.
Sustainable Industry Collaboration – To strengthen long-term partnerships with industries, research bodies, and communities for mutual growth.
