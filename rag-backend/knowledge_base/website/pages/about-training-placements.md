## About Training & Placements

In Agni college, the following successful graduation, students can be placed into internships, jobs, or other placements with the help of the Department of Training and Placement, a specialized department. In order to give students training and career development opportunities, the department must continue working with businesses and organizations, conduct recruitment drives, coordinate with other departments within the institution, and make sure that students have access to the tools and support they need to be successful in their chosen fields.

The ultimate goal of the Department of Placement is to ensure that students have access to a range of career opportunities and are well-prepared to enter the workforce upon graduation.

College internal training programs are essential for preparing students for prosperous jobs. By giving students real-world experience, practical skills, and exposure to real-world problems, these programs are especially made to close the gap between academic knowledge and industry expectations, increasing their employability and industry readiness.

### Company Specific Training Programs

The goal of company-specific training programs is to give pupils a thorough education. These programs are designed to meet the unique requirements of the business and the sector in which it works, guaranteeing that students obtain instruction that is both pertinent and useful. The courses give students a comprehensive grasp of the sector by covering both technical and non-technical topics.

### Key Benefits

Industry-relevant skills
Enhanced employability
Company-specific insights
Improved job readiness

We urge our students to seize this priceless chance to receive training that is customized to meet their needs and is particular to the sector. Students who take part in company-specific training programs will be more equipped to handle the demands of their professions and thrive in the field they have chosen.
