#### An Overview

Agni College of Technology (ACT) is a NAAC A+ Accredited Autonomous Engineering Institution in Chennai, affiliated to Anna University, established in 2001. Recognised as one of the best engineering colleges in Chennai, ACT is AICTE approved and holds NBA accreditation for select departments, offering a premium technical education experience to thousands of students every year.

As an Autonomous Institution, ACT designs its own curriculum, conducts internal examinations and offers semester patterns that give students flexibility and practical depth beyond the standard affiliating university syllabus. With 10+ B.E/B.Tech/M.E programs spanning Computer Science, Electronics, AI & Data Science, Information Technology, Mechanical, Civil, Biomedical, Chemical, EEE and Mechatronics — ACT shapes engineers who are industry-ready on day one.

### Why should you choose Agni College of Technology?

Agni College of Technology is a NAAC A+ Accredited Autonomous Institution affiliated to Anna University recognised as one of the best engineering colleges in Chennai with 25 years of engineering excellence. AICTE approved and NBA accredited, ACT delivers consistent academic quality backed by strong placement records and a curriculum built for the real world.

##### Sports

In Agni College of Technology, students are encouraged to take active part in sports and games. To facilitate this, there is a huge playground for Cricket, FootBall, Tennis, Volleyball, Athletics etc. within the campus.

##### ITSS

Information Technology Services and Systems (ITSS) is the dedicated technology backbone of Agni College of Technology, committed to delivering robust digital infrastructure, technical support, and IT solutions to enhance the teaching, learning, research, and administrative functions of the institution.

##### Gym

At Agni College of Technology, we believe that a healthy body fuels a focused mind. The Agni Fitness Centre (Gym) is a state-of-the-art facility that promotes physical well-being, mental resilience, and a balanced lifestyle among our students, faculty, and staff

##### Library

The Central Library at Agni College of Technology houses nearly 71,670 volumes of books and 9,631 titles that support the students during their study and research. An online journal database with a link to Springer is also available.
