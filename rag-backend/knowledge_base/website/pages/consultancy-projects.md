# Consultancy Projects
Source: https://act.edu.in/consultancy-projects/

## Consultancy Projects

## Objectives

To deliver specialized expertise and solutions to industries and organizations for addressing complex technical and managerial challenges.
To support industries in optimizing their processes, systems, and products, thereby improving operational efficiency and competitiveness.
To facilitate technology transfer from academia to industry by identifying potential commercial applications of academic research.
To assist industries in market research, strategy development, and innovative business model design.

### Roles and Responsibilities of the Consultancy Working Group

To actively engage with industries and foster strong Industry–Institute partnerships.
To create an enabling environment that supports and encourages consultancy activities.
To bridge the gap between academic research and industrial needs through technical collaboration.
To coordinate and streamline all consultancy-related operations for effective implementation.
