## What is TNEA Counselling 2026 in Tamil Nadu?

TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) Counselling is the centralized, single-window admission process for B.E. and B.Tech programs across Tamil Nadu. Conducted by the Department of Technical Education (DoTE), it covers government, government-aided, and self-financing engineering colleges affiliated with Anna University.

The process is entirely merit-based — your Class 12 marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) determine your rank. There is no separate entrance exam. Students are allotted colleges and branches based on their TNEA rank, reservation category, and available seats through tneaonline.org.

⚠️ Important Note TNEA Counselling is the only route for admission to B.E./B.Tech Government Quota seats in Anna University affiliated colleges. Managing your choice-filling strategically is critical for getting into your preferred college and branch.

## Eligibility Criteria for TNEA Counselling 2026

Before applying, make sure you meet all of the following eligibility conditions:

### Academic Qualification

Candidates must have passed Class 12 (HSC) or an equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects. The minimum aggregate required is:

General / OBC Category: 45% aggregate in PCM
SC / ST / SCA Category: 40% aggregate in PCM

### Domicile Requirement

Tamil Nadu native candidates must produce a valid nativity certificate. Candidates from other states who have studied from Class 8 to 12 in Tamil Nadu are also eligible to apply.

### Age Limit

There is no upper age limit for applying to TNEA Counselling 2026. All eligible candidates can apply regardless of age.

## How to Apply for TNEA Counselling 2026 – Step-by-Step

The entire TNEA Counselling 2026 process is conducted online through tneaonline.org. Follow these steps carefully:

1

#### Online Registration at tneaonline.org

Visit the official TNEA portal and click on "New Registration." Enter your name, mobile number, and email ID to create your account. You will receive a User ID and password via SMS and email.

2

#### Fill in the TNEA Application Form 2026

Log in with your credentials. Enter your personal details (name, DOB, gender, contact information), academic details (Class 12 marks, board, year of passing), and school history from Class 7 to 12.

3

#### Enter Special Reservation Details (If Applicable)

If you are eligible for special quotas — Sports, Ex-Servicemen, Differently Abled (PwD), or NRI quota — enter the relevant details and supporting certificate information in this section.

4

#### Upload Required Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required documents in the prescribed format and file size. Ensure images are clear and legible. Incorrect or blurry uploads may lead to application rejection during verification.

5

#### Pay the TNEA Application Fee

Pay the registration fee online through net banking, debit card, credit card, or UPI. The fee is ₹500 for General category and ₹250 for SC/ST/SCA candidates. Save the payment receipt for future reference.

6

#### Preview, Correct, and Submit

Carefully review all entered information. Any errors must be corrected before final submission — changes are not allowed after submission. Once confirmed, submit the TNEA application form 2026.

7

#### Certificate Verification (June 8–20, 2026)

Download a printout of the submitted application. Carry it along with original documents to your nearest TNEA Facilitation Centre for physical verification between June 8 and June 20, 2026.

8

#### Rank List, Choice Filling & Seat Allotment

The rank list is published on June 29, 2026. During choice filling (4th week of July), log in and enter your preferred colleges and branches in order of preference. Seat allotment happens based on your rank, category, and choices. Confirm your allotted seat and complete admission formalities.
