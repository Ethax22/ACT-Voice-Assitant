## Office of Student Affairs

### Welcome to the Office of Student Affairs

At Agni College of Technology Office of Student Affairs (OSA) is committed to creating a vibrant, inclusive, and supportive campus experience. We focus on enhancing student life by providing resources, programs, and services that promote student development, leadership, and well-being.

Whether you need support with housing, career guidance, mental health, student clubs, or organizing events, the OSA is your one-stop destination.

#### Our Mission

To foster a holistic educational environment where students can grow academically, socially, and personally, developing into responsible and dynamic global citizens.
