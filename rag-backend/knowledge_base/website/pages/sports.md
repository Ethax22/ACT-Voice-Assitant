## Sports

In Agni College of Technology, students are encouraged to take active part in sports and games. To facilitate this, there is a huge playground for Cricket, Foot Ball, Tennis, Volley Ball, Athletics etc. within the campus.

An Indoor Stadium built on par with international standards houses games such as Basketball, Badminton, Table Tennis and Squash. Gymnasia are available for boys and girls to exercise and stay fit. The Physical Director supervises and guides all the sports activities in the college.
