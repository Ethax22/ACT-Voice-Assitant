## Library

The Central Library at Agni College of Technology houses nearly 71,670 volumes of books and 9,631 titles that support the students during their study and research. An online journal database with a link to Springer is also available.

The spacious reading area lends a calm and comfortable atmosphere for students and faculty members to pursue excellence in academics. The library is equipped with all modern facilities on par with international standards.

NPTEL video lectures are also available in various disciplines. This facility can be accessed inside the campus through the intranet.

The library functions from 8.00 a.m. to 5.30 p.m and also has a book bank section for needy students.
