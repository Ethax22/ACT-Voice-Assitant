## Controller of Examinations

## Preamble

The Office of the Controller of Examinations (CoE) at Agni College of Technology was established in 2024, in accordance with the UGC Regulations for Autonomous Institutions, after the college was conferred with autonomy status. Since its inception, the office has been dedicated to ensuring fairness, transparency, and efficiency in the conduct of all academic examinations.

The CoE plays a vital role in upholding the academic standards of the institution by administering examinations, evaluating student performance, and facilitating the timely declaration of results.

## Functions of the CoE Office

Preparing and publishing the academic examination schedule.
Conducting end-semester and internal examinations in a systematic manner.
Coordinating with academic departments for question paper setting and moderation.
Arranging for central valuation and ensuring timely publication of results.
Issuing grade sheets, transcripts, and provisional certificates.
Ensuring strict adherence to examination regulations and maintaining confidentiality.
Addressing student queries and grievances related to examinations.

#### Vision

To establish a transparent, efficient, and student-centric examination system that reflects academic excellence and prepares graduates for future challenges.

#### Mission

To conduct examinations with fairness and integrity.
To ensure timely evaluation and result processing.
To continuously improve examination practices through technology and innovation.

## Student Information

Exam Time Tables – Published on the student portal and notice boards.
Hall Tickets – Available prior to examinations through the CoE office.
Results – Announced on the official college website and portals.
Revaluation/Re-totaling – Students can apply within the prescribed dates after result publication.
Certificates & Transcripts – Issued upon request through the CoE office.
