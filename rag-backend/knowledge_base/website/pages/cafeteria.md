## Cafeteria

At Agni College of Technology, there is a well-equipped, modern cafeteria on the campus providing hygienic food at subsidized rates for the students, staff, and visitors to the college.

The cafeteria has become essential to improve the quality of education and ensure the faculty and students do not stay hungry and eat healthy at an affordable cost.

The cafeteria is open for extended hours for those inclined for a quick snack amidst their various activities.
