## Centres of Excellence

### Objective

The CoEs at Agni College of Technology are playing a vital role in preparing students for the industry and in promoting innovation, skill development and entrepreneurship.

The objectives of the Centers of Excellence (CoE) at Agni College of Technology are to

Provide students with the latest industry-relevant knowledge and skills.
Bridge the academia-industry gap by providing opportunities for students to work on live projects with industry partners.
Help companies to test new ideas, processes, and technologies before implementation.
Promote innovation and entrepreneurship among students and faculty.
Expose students to the latest trends and technologies in their field of study.
Promote research and development in emerging areas of engineering and technology.
Incubate startups and promote entrepreneurship.
Collaborate with industry partners on joint research projects.

### Context

The Centers of Excellence (CoE) at Agni College of Technology are specialized units that provide students and faculty with access to the latest industry-relevant knowledge, skills, and equipment. The CoEs are established in collaboration with leading industry partners, and they offer students the opportunity to work on live projects and to gain insights into the latest trends and technologies in their field of study.

The CoEs at Agni College of Technology are an integral part of the college’s commitment to providing students with a high-quality education that prepares them for success in the global economy. The CoEs help students to develop the skills and knowledge they need to be successful in their careers, and they also contribute to the advancement of knowledge and innovation in engineering and technology. For establishing the CoE the MoU is signed between Agni college of Technologies and other leading Industries

### Harita TVS CoE

The collaboration between Haritha TVS (Harita Techserv) and Agni College of Technology (ACT) is a strategic partnership designed to enhance the technical proficiency of students in the Mechanical, Mechatronics, and Automobile Engineering departments. This alliance centers on a dedicated Center of Excellence (CoE) that bridges the gap between academic learning and automotive industry requirements.

Sustainable Mobility Focus:The CoE specifically targets the development of sustainable and energy-efficient vehicular solutions, aligning with the global shift toward green technology and electric mobility.
Design & Research:Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard CAD/CAM/CAE tools used by the TVS Group. The facility functions as a research hub for new product development, allowing students to work on live engineering projects.
Skill Development:Harita Techserv provides specialized training programs that prepare students for high-end roles in design engineering. This includes the Campus-to-Career initiative, which often leads to direct placements within Harita’s offshore development centers or the broader TVS ecosystem.

By integrating TVS’s engineering rigor with ACT’s academic environment, this partnership ensures that graduates are well-versed in the practical challenges of modern vehicle manufacturing and design.

### Data Patterns (India) Limited CoE

Data Patterns (India) Limited is a prominent recruiter and industry partner for Agni College of Technology (ACT), Chennai. Recruitment & Packages:Data Patterns is known for hiring “fresh engineers” for core functional roles. While the college’s average package is around 25–4.4 LPA, core recruiters like Data Patterns often offer competitive entry-level roles for specialized electronics design and manufacturing.

Proximity:Located in the Siruseri SIPCOT IT Park, Data Patterns is geographically close to the college, facilitating regular campus drives, guest lectures, and internship opportunities for ACT students.

This partnership underscores ACT’s success in placing students in “niche” core industries beyond standard IT services, maintaining a consistent placement rate of above 90% for eligible candidates.
