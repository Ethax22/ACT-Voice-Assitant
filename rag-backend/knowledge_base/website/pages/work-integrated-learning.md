Process

## WIL Partnership Process – What Happens Next?

1
MoU & Introduction: The ACT management team visits your organization by appointment, presents the WIL program details to your authorities, and formalizes the partnership through a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU).

2
Student Selection: Industry partners brief 2nd semester students about their organization and select 5–15 students based on minor assessment (if required) as per the agreed learning framework — deep learning or broad learning.

3
Mentoring & Evaluation: Students work alongside an identified industry mentor throughout the WIL program. At the end of each activity, the mentor also acts as evaluator, assigning credits or points based on expected outcomes.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions – WIL at Agni College

What is Work Integrated Learning (WIL) at Agni College of Technology? ▼
Work Integrated Learning (WIL) at Agni College of Technology is a structured, industry-immersive program beginning from Semester 2 and continuing through all 8 semesters of the bachelor's degree. It guarantees 12–16 months of real industry experience — via internships, apprenticeships, on-the-job training, and industry projects — across all engineering programs. Agni is an autonomous institution conferred by UGC and Anna University.
Which semesters does the WIL curriculum cover? ▼
WIL covers all 8 semesters: Industry Practices (Sem II, 4–6 weeks, 10 pts), Industry Training I (Sem III, 6–8 weeks, 15 pts), Industry Training II (Sem IV, 6–8 weeks, 15 pts), Industrial Problem Solving 1 (Sem V, 6–8 weeks, 30 pts), Industrial Problem Solving 2 (Sem VI, 6–8 weeks, 30 pts), Mini Project / Internship (Sem VII, 4 weeks, 2 credits), and Semester Long Internship (Sem VIII, 12 weeks, 10 credits).
Is WIL available for all engineering programs at Agni? ▼
Yes. Work Integrated Learning is assured across all bachelor's degree programs at Agni College of Technology, including B.E. CSE, ECE, EEE, Civil Engineering, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Biomedical, B.Tech IT, Chemical Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence & Data Science.
How can an industry partner collaborate with Agni College for WIL? ▼
Industries can collaborate through internships, industry-offered courses, research collaborations, expert mentorship, curriculum reviews, value-added courses, Centres of Excellence, and more. The ACT management team will visit your organization to sign an MoU and formalize the collaboration.
What are the outcomes of WIL for Agni College students? ▼
Students graduate with 12–16 months of verified industry experience. They are no longer freshers but domain-experienced professionals, boosting their employability and readiness for dynamic workplace environments across India and globally.
