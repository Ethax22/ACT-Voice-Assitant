## Agni MSME Incubation centre

MSME INCUBATION CELL

“Fostering Innovation and Technological Growth for National Empowerment”

The Ministry of Education, Government of India, established the Institution’s Innovation Council (IIC) on November 21, 2018, with the goal of cultivating a culture of innovation within educational institutions. Agni College of Technology is honored to be selected as a leading center in building an innovation ecosystem on campus. This initiative enables the college to inspire and support young students, transforming their innovative ideas into functional prototypes, paving the way for entrepreneurship.

Through this initiative, the Government of India offers innovators the opportunity to develop and nurture their groundbreaking ideas into market-ready products. Since its launch in 2008, the Government has provided financial assistance covering 75% to 85% of project costs, up to a maximum of ₹8.00 Lakh, for commercialization through Business Incubators (BIS).

### Key Focus Areas of MSME

Promoting emerging technology-based innovative ventures by sourcing ideas from professionals.
Supporting untapped creativity from individual innovators.
Encouraging innovators to become technology-driven entrepreneurs.
Facilitating networking and fostering connections within the broader innovation ecosystem.

### Benefits for Students

The MSME Incubation Cell at Agni College of Technology provides both financial and technical support to foster innovative ideas from engineering students, technocrats, and industrialists.

### Agni College of Technology: MSME Incubation Centre

The Agni College of Technology MSME Incubation Centre is recognized by the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India. The centre provides critical support to students, helping them validate their ideas and create working prototypes.
The Agni College of Technology MSME Incubation Centre is spread over 2000 sq. ft. and provides:
A modern workspace for incubators.
Communication and computing facilities.
Equipment for prototype development.
Library and training center access.
Assistance with company registration and patent filing.
Travel and food allowances for selected projects.

The centre also organizes awareness programs and networking events to connect students with industry leaders and venture capitalists.

### Start-up Programme at Agni College of Technology

#### Our Vision

To create 100 student-owned tech startups and generate 7,500 employment opportunities within the next 5 years. This vision will be achieved by establishing a strong entrepreneurial ecosystem supported by the institution.

#### Our Mission

To help students launch their startups on campus, thus contributing to the economic and social development of the region. The strategies include:

Motivating students to pursue entrepreneurship as a career.
Preparing students for successful startup launches.
Creating customized training materials and pre-startup activities for students.
Organizing capacity-building activities for both students and faculty.

### What is a Start-up?

A startup is a business model focused on product or service innovation, designed to be scalable and sustainable.

### Advisory Committee on Start-ups:

The Advisory Committee, comprising academic leaders and industry experts, provides guidance to students in developing innovative products and ideas.
