## Startups

### Promoting Student Entrepreneurship through UDYAM Registration

Empowering Students with MSME Recognition and Real-World Business Opportunities

Agni College of Technology (ACT) has emerged as a front-runner in promoting student entrepreneurship by actively encouraging and facilitating UDYAM registration for student-led ventures under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India. This initiative is part of ACT’s broader mission to instill business readiness, innovation, and self-reliance among its students, aligning with national priorities like Startup India and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

Through formal registration under the UDYAM scheme, students are able to convert their innovative ideas into recognized micro enterprises, gaining access to a wide range of government benefits, support mechanisms, and institutional backing. This initiative also provides tangible outcomes that go beyond the classroom, equipping students with practical business experience and entrepreneurial confidence.

### Diverse Sectors, Real-World Solutions

Student-run startups registered under UDYAM span a variety of sectors, reflecting the diversity and interdisciplinary strength of the college:

Event Management and Coordination Services – Startups offering corporate event planning, college festival management, and cultural coordination across districts.
AI-driven Educational Platforms – Technology-enabled learning tools and content delivery systems tailored to school and college learners.
Textile Fibre Innovation – Startups focusing on the use of sustainable and alternate fibres for fabric manufacturing.
Semi-processed Pulse-Based Products – Ventures developing nutritious, ready-to-cook food items from pulses to promote healthy eating.

These startups have officially received UDYAM registration numbers and are classified under the National Industry Classification (NIC) system for the academic year 2024–25. Each venture falls under either the ‘Services’ or ‘Manufacturing’ activity categories, making them eligible for relevant MSME schemes and benefits.

### Geographical Reach and Institutional Linkages

Though incubated and mentored at Agni College of Technology, many of these ventures are now operating across various districts of Tamil Nadu, including Chennai, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli, Madurai, and Tiruchirappalli. This demonstrates the state-wide entrepreneurial impact of ACT’s incubation programs and how students are expanding their businesses beyond the college campus.

The strong link between ACT and these startups continues post-registration. Faculty members and alumni entrepreneurs provide:

Ongoing mentorship
Business model refinement
Support for applying to government schemes and subsidies
Connections to marketing and logistics networks

### Benefits of UDYAM Registration for Student Startups

By completing UDYAM registration, these student-led ventures now qualify for a host of MSME benefits, including:

Funding assistance through government-backed loans and grants
Incubation and mentorship support under MSME development schemes
Access to public procurement and e-marketplace (GeM) listings
Eligibility for intellectual property subsidies and credit guarantees
Priority consideration in startup contests and innovation fairs

These benefits greatly enhance the sustainability and scalability of student ventures and prepare them for long-term market entry and competitiveness.

### A Culture of Entrepreneurship and Self-Reliance

ACT’s initiative to promote UDYAM registration reflects a deep institutional commitment to experiential learning and entrepreneurial empowerment. By providing a framework where students can formally launch their own enterprises while still in college, ACT:

Encourages hands-on innovation and business thinking
Bridges the gap between technical education and entrepreneurial execution
Strengthens student participation in the national startup and MSME ecosystem

This real-world exposure transforms students from learners into founders, problem solvers, and contributors to the economy.

Through UDYAM registration and the active support of its Entrepreneurship Development Cell, Agni College of Technology continues to create a generation of self-reliant, startup-ready graduates who are not only industry-ready but are also capable of leading and shaping the industries of tomorrow.
