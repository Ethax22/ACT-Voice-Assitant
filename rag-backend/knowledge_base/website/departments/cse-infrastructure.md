### Infrastructure

### Cutting-Edge Laboratories & Facilities

The Department imparts world-class training and provides state of the art computing facilities for the students to learn and research. It also promotes active industry-institute collaboration by identifying areas of interest and taking part in sponsored research projects and consultancy services.

Some of the major research areas which the faculty members and students are working on include Networks, Database, Theoretical Computer Science, Multimedia, Image Processing, Software Engineering and Data Mining.

The department provides extensive computing resources for research and education. This includes more than 300 high-performance computers in the range of Linux, Windows workstations and a good number of laptop systems. In addition to the general file server, the department maintains two specialized servers for database clients and Linux based workstations. The network communication at the department includes a fiber optic backbone, offering 16 Mbps connectivity to the Internet, and provides Intranet within the hi-tech lab, which hosts 11 laboratories. The department has wireless internet connectivity via a campus-wide 802.11b network to the laptops.

### Laboratory Facilities

The Department is well equipped with 11 diversified laboratories including a dedicated laboratory which is installed with a variety of software catering to the needs of the students and covering the curriculum specified by the affiliated University

### Seminar Hall

To conduct special seminars covering the latest developments and trends in the field of Computer Science, a fully equipped Seminar Hall with a seating capacity of 120 is available in the department.

### Class Rooms

Each classroom is Wi-Fi enabled and furnished with an OHP, one LCD projector, and a dedicated Laptop to support the e-Learning process.

### Library

The department library located in a dedicated and spacious room has more than 1000 books covering a wide range of subjects, and several journals that include ACM Transactions on Database Systems, Software Engineering and Methodology, Operating Systems and Programming Languages, ACM Computing Surveys, International Journal for Neural Systems and Computational Intelligence and Applications, Software Engineering Notes, Operating System Review, Computer Communication Review and Computer Architecture.
