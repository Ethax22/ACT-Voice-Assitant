ANNUAL REPORT – INTERNAL COMPLAINTS COMMITTEE (ICC)
In compliance with the directives of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India and the guidelines issued by AICTE, Agni College of Technology has constituted an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) to promote gender sensitization and to prevent, prohibit, and redress complaints related to sexual harassment within the institution.
The ICC is entrusted with the responsibility of addressing any form of discriminatory behavior, including incidents of sexual harassment that are reported or brought to its notice. The committee also recommends appropriate remedial and disciplinary actions against offenders, in accordance with established norms and regulations.
The committee comprises representatives from the faculty, staff, and student community. The ICC is constituted as follows:
1. Dr. Srinivasan Alavandar – Principal, Chairman
2. Dr. L. Balaji Madhavan – HOD, CSE, Member
3. Dr. Sureka N – HOD, BME, Member
4. Dr.Raghu.Y - HOD, S&H, Member
5. Dr. Ratheesh R – HOD, ECE, Member
6. Dr. Narendranaathan – HOD, AERO, Member
7. Dr. R. Pandiyarajan – HOD, MHT, Member
8. Dr. P. Purushothaman – HOD, MECH, Member
9. Dr. P. Murugan – HOD, CHEM, Member
10. Dr.Simi Margarat – HOD, IT, Member
11. Mr.K.Ilayaperumal – Faculty Member
12. Ms. R. Rajitha – Faculty Member
Details of the ICC Meeting:
The Internal Complaints Committee meeting was held on 16 August 2021 at Agni College of Technology, Chennai. The following points were discussed during the meeting:
- The Presiding Officer welcomed all members and briefed them on the background, constitution, scope, and objectives of the ICC.
- Copies of the AICTE Regulations applicable to all institutions were circulated, and key clauses relevant to the institute were discussed in detail.
- The committee resolved to redraft the existing Policy against Sexual Harassment. Based on the suggestions and inputs provided by the committee members, the revised policy was finalized and approved.
The meeting concluded with a consensus on strengthening awareness, compliance, and implementation of ICC guidelines to ensure a safe and inclusive campus environment.
