HIGHERSTUDIESAND CAREERGUIDANCE
VISION
Toempower students withglobal competitiveness byprovidingcontinuous guidance, training,
and opportunities for higher education, research, entrepreneurship, and successful career
pathways.
MISSION
 To create awareness on national and international higher education opportunities,
competitiveexams,scholarships,andresearchprograms.
 To provide personalized mentoring and counselling through experts, industry
professionals,and alumni.
 To enhance student employability through structured training in communication,
aptitude,technical skills,andprofessional ethics.
 To collaborate with industries and universities to facilitate internships, projects, and
global learningexposure.
OBJECTIVES
 To facilitate student progression into higher education, professional employment,
entrepreneurship,andstart-up initiatives through structured guidance andsupport.
 To enhance student success in national and international competitive examinations
such as GATE, GRE, TOEFL, IELTS, CAT, Civil Services, and other relevant
qualifyingtests byprovidingcoachingresources, mentoring, andawarenessprograms.
 To ensure maximum student engagement and participation in career development
activities, including skill-based training programs, counselling sessions, workshops,
internships,andnetworkingopportunities.
OUTCOMES
Studentswill:
 Gainclarity onhigher educationandcareerpathways.
 Improve aptitude,communication,andinterview skills.
 Becomeindustry-readyand globallycompetent graduates.
