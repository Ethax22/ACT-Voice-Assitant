### Student Disciplinary Policy
### Preamble
Agni College of Technology is committed to provide a safe, respectful, and conducive environment for learning, research, and holistic development. Discipline is integral to this mission, fostering a sense of responsibility, integrity, and respect among students. This policy outlines the code of conduct, disciplinary procedures, and potential consequences for violations.
### 1. Philosophy of Discipline
The primary goal of the disciplinary process is educational and corrective, not merely punitive. The Institute believes in fostering an environment of trust and responsibility. While the majority of students are expected to uphold the highest standards of conduct, the disciplinary mechanism exists to address instances of misconduct fairly, promptly, and consistently, ensuring the protection of the rights and welfare of the entire student community.
### 2. Code of Student Conduct
All students of Agni College of Technology are expected to adhere to the following rules of conduct, both within and outside the campus, in a manner that upholds the institution's reputation.
### A. General Conduct & Respect:
- Students must behave with dignity, honesty, and integrity.
- Show respect to faculty, staff, security personnel, visitors, and fellow students.
- Maintain decorum in academic blocks, administrative offices, and public spaces.
### B. Prohibition of Ragging & Harassment:
- Ragging in any form—physical, verbal, or psychological—is a criminal offence and is strictly prohibited. Failure to report ragging incidents is also a punishable offence.
- Any form of harassment, bullying, or discrimination based on race, religion, caste, gender, ethnicity, or personal appearance is prohibited.
### C. Academic Integrity:
- Students must avoid all forms of academic misconduct, including plagiarism, cheating, copying, and any other deceptive practices in examinations or assignments.
- Submission of fraudulent documents or false information for any academic or administrative purpose is strictly prohibited.
### D. Property and Environment:
- Students shall not damage, deface, or destroy institute property, public property, or the property of others.
- The campus is tobacco-free, alcohol-free, and drug-free. Possession, consumption, or distribution of intoxicating substances is prohibited.
- Littering is prohibited; students must use designated waste bins and contribute to keeping the campus clean.
### E. Use of Technology and Media:
- Unauthorized audio/video recording in classrooms, labs, or private zones is prohibited.
- Students must not communicate with or invite media representatives onto the campus without prior permission from the competent authority.
- Cyber offenses, including hacking, unauthorized access, and online harassment, misuse of social media are serious misconducts.

### 3. Disciplinary Committees and Accountability
To address different levels of indiscipline, the following committee is constituted:
| Committee | Accountability | Composition |
| Disciplinary Committee (DC) | Major and Minor misconducts, repeat misconducts, and cases with institute-wide/department/classroom/hostel/campus misconduct. | Dean Academics, Dean Administration, Two Senior Faculty Members, Head and Faculty Advisor of the concerned department, Warden for Hostel |
### 4. Disciplinary Procedure
1. Reporting: Any incident can be reported by students, faculty, or staff.
2. Preliminary Review & Investigation: The relevant committee (DC)investigates the matter promptly, gathering evidence and recording statements from all involved parties.
3. Hearing: The accused student(s) and the complainant are given a fair chance to present their case.
4. Recommendation: The committee deliberates and submits a written report with findings and recommended action to the principal.
5. Final Decision: The principal reviews the recommendation and imposes the final punishment. The decision is communicated in writing to the student and their parents/guardians.
6. Appeal: A student may appeal the decision to the principal within 7 days of receiving the order. The principal's decision on the appeal is final.

### 6. Nature of Involvement & Punishments in Group misconducts
The severity of the punishment is determined by the nature of the offense and the level of involvement of the student:
• Nature of Involvement:
o Instigator/Leader: The one who plans, initiates, or leads the misconduct.
o Active Participant: One who willingly takes part in the act.
o Accomplice/Aider: One who assists or facilitates the act.
- Non-Reporter: One who, with knowledge of a serious offense (like ragging), fails to report it.

### 7. General Notes on Penalties
- Repeat Offenders: For a second or subsequent misconduct, the punishment will be at least one step higher than the previous penalty.
- False Complaints: Any student found to have made a maliciously false complaint will be penalized with a punishment one step higher than what would have been imposed for the alleged misconduct.
- Monetary Fines: All fines are to be paid within a stipulated time frame.
- Parental Involvement: For all penalties from P3 onwards, parental intimation (P2) is mandatory.
### Disclaimer:
- This policy serves as a guideline. The Disciplinary Committee (DC) and the principal reserve the right to interpret rules and impose penalties based on the specific circumstances and severity of each case. The Institute may revise or amend this policy as and when necessary.
- If the nature of the misconduct has more than one recommended punishment (P-Codes), the punishment may be applied individually, in combination, or at the maximum level, depending on the recommendations of the Disciplinary Committee (DC).
