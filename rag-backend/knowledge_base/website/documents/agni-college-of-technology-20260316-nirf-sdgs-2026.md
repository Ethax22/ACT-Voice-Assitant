Activity Details: The institution has taken several initiatives to reduce energy consumption and promote a sustainable campus environment. Solar power systems have been installed on selected buildings to generate renewable energy and reduce dependence on
conventional electricity. Solar street lights are also used in some parts of the campus.
Most of the conventional lights in classrooms, laboratories, corridors and offices have been replaced with LED lights, which consume less electricity and have a longer life. Energy-efficient electrical equipment and star-rated appliances are also used wherever
possible.
The institution conducts periodic energy audits to monitor electricity consumption and identify areas where energy can be saved. Based on the recommendations, necessary improvements are implemented to reduce wastage.
Campus buildings are designed to make use of natural lighting and ventilation so that the need for artificial lighting and cooling is minimized during daytime.
