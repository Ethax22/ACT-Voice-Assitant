"Exploíing Diveígent Thinking: A Mind Mapping Session by 3íd-Yeaí Students"
The 3rd-year students engaged in an insightful session on Divergent Thinking, where they created mind
maps to visualize creative problem-solving, idea generation, and innovative thinking processes. Their
mind maps showcased unique perspectives, brainstorming techniques, and connections between ideas,
highlighting the power of thinking beyond conventional boundaries. This session emphasized the
importance of open-ended exploration and creativity in tackling complex problems.

## Page 4

The maps showcased how students branched out their thoughts in multiple directions, emphasizing:
1. Idca Gckcíatiok – Techniques like brainstorming, random word association, and SCAMPER
were widely used to foster creativity.
2. Píoblcm-Solvikg – Students explored unconventional solutions to problems, considering
multiple possibilities rather than linear thinking.
3. Ikkovatiok G Cícativity – The maps highlighted how students linked diverse concepts to
develop unique ideas.
4. Collaboíatiok – Many mind maps illustrated teamwork, where students built upon each
other’s thoughts, expanding their thinking capacity.
5. Rcal-Woíld Applicatioks – Examples from industries like design, entrepreneurship, and
engineering were incorporated to showcase the relevance of divergent thinking.
Overall, the mind maps demonstrated how students embraced open-ended exploration, leading to
innovative ideas and multiple solutions to a given challenge.
