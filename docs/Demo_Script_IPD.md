# Agni AI — Demo Video Script (under 3 minutes)

Three short screen recordings, about 55 seconds each. Join them into one video, or send them as three clips.

**Before recording:** use headphones and record the computer's sound along with your mic. Do one practice call first (the first call of the day can be slow). Keep answers short — if the bot gives a long answer, just talk over it, which shows interruption works.

---

## Clip 1 — Website widget (~55 s)

Open `https://agni-agent.centralindia.cloudapp.azure.com/`, click **Agni AI**.

| # | You | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Type: `What B.E. courses do you offer?` | Typed chat |
| 2 | Click **Let's Talk**, wait for the greeting | Voice |
| 3 | Say: "How are the placements here?" | Spoken answer |
| 4 | Say: "Hostel facility iruka?" | Replies in Tanglish on its own |
